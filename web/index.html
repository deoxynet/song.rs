Transfer troubleshooting

Place the devices within 1 metre, point the speaker toward the microphone, and avoid covering either one. If copies keep failing, move closer before raising the volume.

On a phone call, hold the receiving device near the earpiece or speaker. Disabling the call app’s noise suppression can help.
